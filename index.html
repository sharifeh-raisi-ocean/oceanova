06 / A CHANGING PLANET

Why ocean science matters.

Understanding ocean processes is essential to informed decisions about a changing climate, healthy coasts and a sustainable relationship with the sea.

01

Climate understanding

The ocean absorbs and redistributes vast amounts of heat and carbon, influencing the global climate system.

02

Coastal resilience

Ocean knowledge helps communities understand coastal change, hazards, erosion and changing sea levels.

03

Marine biodiversity

Research supports the protection and restoration of habitats and the species that depend on them.

04

Sustainable decisions

Reliable observations and research inform responsible management of marine resources and ecosystems.
